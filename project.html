From a photo to a plan, in four steps

The same pipeline runs whether the request comes from a phone in a Vidarbha cotton field or a district office dashboard in Pune.

  1. 1

    Capture

    A farmer photographs the affected crop, or a sensor logs an unusual reading — no special equipment required.

  2. 2

    Combine

    The image is checked against local weather, soil, location and known outbreaks nearby, not looked at alone.

  3. 3

    Diagnose

    A model trained on Maharashtra's crop diseases returns a likely cause with a confidence score and a suggested treatment.

  4. 4

    Confirm & act

    An extension worker can validate the result in unclear cases before it reaches the farmer as a plain-language alert.

Six sources feed every diagnosis

A single photo rarely tells the whole story. AgriShield brings the surrounding context in automatically.

Crop images

Photos from a phone, a field camera, or an optional drone pass over the plot.

Pest traps & sensors

Pheromone traps, soil and humidity sensors, and notes a worker logs by hand.

Weather

Temperature, humidity, rainfall and a short-range forecast for the exact plot.

Location & land

GPS position, land use, and district-level disease history recorded over time.

Crop & farm details

Crop type, growth stage, soil condition and the plot's own pest history.

Expert knowledge base

A reference library of diseases, agronomy practice and Maharashtra Krishi Vibhag advisories.

What the farmer sees

A result written for the field, not for a lab report

Every diagnosis comes back as a short, plain-language card: what the problem likely is, how confident the system is, and what to do about it today.

Downy mildew — grapevine Confidence: 88%

Yellow patches on the upper leaf surface, consistent with the humid spell reported around Nashik this week.

Remove affected leaves Apply a copper-based spray Re-check in 5 days

Unclear cases are automatically routed to an extension worker for a second opinion before any advice reaches the farmer.
