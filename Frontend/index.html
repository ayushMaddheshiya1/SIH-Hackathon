From a photo to a plan, in four steps

The same pipeline runs whether the request comes from a phone in a paddy field or a dashboard in a district office.

  1. 1

    Capture

    A farmer photographs the affected crop, or a sensor logs an unusual reading — no special equipment required.

  2. 2

    Combine

    The image is checked against weather, soil, location and known local outbreaks, not looked at alone.

  3. 3

    Diagnose

    A model trained on regional crop diseases returns a likely cause with a confidence score and a suggested treatment.

  4. 4

    Confirm & act

    An extension worker can validate the result in unclear cases before it reaches the farmer as a plain-language alert.

Six sources feed every diagnosis

A single photo rarely tells the whole story. KrishiMitra brings the surrounding context in automatically.

Crop images

Photos from a phone, a field camera, or an optional drone pass over the plot.

Pest traps & sensors

Pheromone traps, soil and humidity sensors, and notes a worker logs by hand.

Weather

Temperature, humidity, rainfall and a short-range forecast for the exact plot.

Location & land

GPS position, land use, and disease history recorded for that area over time.

Crop & farm details

Crop type, growth stage, soil condition and the plot's own pest history.

Expert knowledge base

A reference library of diseases, agronomy practice and current advisories.

What the farmer sees

A result written for the field, not for a lab report

Every diagnosis comes back as a short, plain-language card: what the problem likely is, how confident the system is, and what to do about it today.

Early blight — tomato Confidence: 88%

Dark concentric spots on lower leaves, consistent with recent humid conditions in your area.

Remove affected leaves Apply copper-based spray Re-check in 5 days

Unclear cases are automatically routed to an extension worker for a second opinion before any advice reaches the farmer.

One platform, three very different days

The same data moves through interfaces built for the person using it — a field, a clipboard, or a district map.

Farmer

Upload a photo, get a diagnosis, and keep a running record of crop health across every season.

  • Upload an image or a short report
  • View diagnosis and treatment advice
  • Get alerts before an outbreak spreads
  • Track crop health over time

Extension worker

Step in exactly where the model is unsure, and keep every field visit on record.

  • Review field reports from nearby farms
  • Confirm or correct uncertain diagnoses
  • Send verified advice back to farmers
  • See alerts across the whole area

Government

See outbreak patterns forming at district or state level while there's still time to respond.

  • State and district-level disease maps
  • Reports and trend analytics
  • Resource allocation planning
  • Policy and advisory planning support
